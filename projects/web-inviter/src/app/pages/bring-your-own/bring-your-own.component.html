Your design goes out exactly as you made it

We won't change anything inside it. Every guest sees the identical artwork — the same names, the same words, the same colours. We can't make an uploaded design personal per guest the way our own templates are.

What you still get
  • Their name in the message. The email or Viber that carries the invitation greets each guest personally, even though the design itself doesn't change.
  • A private link each. Every guest gets their own, and the same protections as any invitation here — nobody can forward their way into someone else's.
  • RSVP. Added under your design, so guests can reply without you chasing them.
  • Your guest list and delivery. Import it, send it, and see who opened what.
  • A media bucket. The camera on the night and everything anyone shoots, collected in one place.

Your design is yours. It is never added to our gallery or shown to anyone else.
