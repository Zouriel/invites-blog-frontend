Your design goes out exactly as you made it

We won't change anything in it. Every guest sees exactly the same picture, with the same names, words and colours. Unlike our own designs, an uploaded design can't show each guest their own name.

What you still get
  • One link for everyone. You don't need a guest list. Paste one short link wherever you like.
  • Or a link for each guest. Add a guest list and every guest gets their own link, which only works for them.
  • Their name in the message. With a guest list, the email that carries the invitation uses each guest's name, even though the design stays the same.
  • Replies. Guests on your list get a reply button under your design. People who open the shared link aren't asked to reply.
  • Photos from the day. A camera button on the invitation, and every photo your guests take kept in one place.

Your design is yours. It is never added to our gallery or shown to anyone else.
