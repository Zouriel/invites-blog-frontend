Not a picture. An invitation that moves.

Most digital invitations are an image, a PDF or a video, and every guest gets the same one. An invites.blog invitation is built in HTML, like a small website. It animates as it opens and as your guests scroll, shows each person their own name and the details meant for them, and takes their reply in one tap.

On the day of the event the same invitation opens a camera. There is nothing to install, and every photo and video your guests take goes straight into your event's album.

Already have a design as a picture or video? Upload it and you still get the guest list, replies and photos.
