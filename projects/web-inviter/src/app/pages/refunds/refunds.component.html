Delivery

What you buy is delivered to your account online, as soon as the bank confirms your payment, usually within a minute:

  • A Gold or Platinum pass switches on for the event it was bought for, and covers it for a year.
  • Another year of a pass extends that event's cover by a year.
  • "Keep your photos" keeps the event's albums online for another year.
  • Emailed invitations are added to the event's sending allowance.
  • Premium, Photographer and Studio switch on for your account for the month paid for, and each automatic renewal extends it the moment it's paid.
  • The AI designer add-on switches on for your account as soon as it is paid for, and stays on with each renewal of Premium.

You'll see it on the event's dashboard and on the Billing page, and the payment is listed under Billing. If something you paid for hasn't appeared within an hour, contact us with the date and amount and we'll sort it out.

There are no import, export or customs restrictions or duties, because nothing is shipped.

Refunds

Refunds are limited. Please note these before you pay:

  • A pass, another year of a pass, or "Keep your photos" can be refunded in full until guests start adding photos to the event or invitations are sent under it. After that it is not refundable.
  • Invitations we have already emailed for you are not refundable.
  • Premium, Photographer and Studio can be cancelled at any time. They keep working until the end of the period already paid for, which is not refunded.
  • The AI designer add-on can be turned off at any time. It keeps working until the end of the month already paid for, and what was paid for it, including a charge for part of a month, is not refunded.
  • If you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or what you paid for never arrived, you get a full refund.
How to ask for a refund

Email {{ company.email }} or call {{ company.phone }} within 14 days of paying. Tell us the account's email, the event or plan it was for, and the date and amount of the payment. We reply within 3 working days. An approved refund goes back to the card you paid with, in rufiyaa. How long it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank.

Disputing a payment with your bank instead of contacting us may suspend the pass or plan it paid for while the dispute is open.

Automatic renewal and cancelling

A pass is paid once and does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

Premium, Photographer and Studio renew automatically at the end of each month. The bank keeps the card you paid with on file, and we charge it at the price in force at the time; we email you a receipt each time. The review screen says this before you pay. If a renewal doesn't go through, we try again the next day and email you; after three tries, automatic renewal stops.

Premium is MVR 77 a month. The AI designer is an optional add-on to Premium for MVR 77 more a month, so Premium with the AI designer renews at MVR 154 a month; Studio includes it. Photographer is MVR 540 a month. If you add the AI designer partway through a month of Premium you have already paid for, you are charged straight away only for the days left in that month (the days left × MVR 77 ÷ 30, rounded up to a whole rufiyaa, and at least MVR 5), and from then on it renews automatically together with Premium. If you had Premium on 13 November 2026, when the AI designer was still included, you keep it until the end of the period you have already paid for; Premium then renews at MVR 77 without it, and you can add it from Billing.

To cancel, turn off automatic renewal on the Billing page of your account, at any time, or contact us. Nothing more is charged, and the plan keeps working until the end of the period already paid for, which is not refunded. To stop only the AI designer, turn it off on the Billing page: it keeps working until the end of the month already paid for, and Premium then renews at MVR 77 without it. You can also stop using the service and delete your events at any time.

Bought in the app

Passes, "Keep your photos", emailed invitations, Premium, Photographer and Studio bought in the invites.blog app are paid to Google (Google Play) or Apple (the App Store), at the price the store shows you, and delivered to your account the same way as above. In the stores, Premium is US$4.99 a month and "Premium + AI designer" is US$9.99 a month and Photographer is US$34.99 a month, or the equivalent the store shows in your currency.

  • Refunds are decided by Google or Apple under their own policies, and we can't refund a store purchase ourselves. Ask Google through your Play account's order history, or Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. When the store refunds a purchase, what it paid for is withdrawn.
  • Premium (with or without the AI designer), Photographer and Studio bought in the app renew monthly through the store and are charged to your Google or Apple account, unless you turn off automatic renewal at least 24 hours before the current month ends. Manage or cancel them in your Google Play or Apple account's subscription settings; our Billing page can't stop them. They keep working until the end of the month already paid for. Adding the AI designer is an upgrade the store charges for under its own rules; removing it takes effect at the next renewal.
  • If something you bought in the app hasn't arrived within an hour, contact us with the date and the store's order number, and we'll sort it out.
