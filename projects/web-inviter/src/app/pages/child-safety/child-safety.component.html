Legal Child safety standards invites.blog does not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) anywhere on our website or in our app.

These standards apply to the invites.blog website and the invites.blog app, run by {{ company.legalName }}, trading as {{ company.tradingName }}.

What we don't allow

Nobody may use invites.blog to create, upload, store, share or ask for:

  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM), in any form, including drawn or computer-generated images;
  • sexual content involving anyone under 18, or content that sexualises a child;
  • grooming, or any contact with a child for a sexual purpose;
  • threats or blackmail against a child, including sextortion;
  • trafficking or any other sexual exploitation of a child.
Who can have an account

You must be 16 or older to create an account or sign in. Our Terms say so.

How to report a concern

Write to {{ company.email }} with "Child safety" in the subject. In the app, the same address is under Settings, Contact us. Tell us the event, album or link, and what you saw. Please don't send us copies of the images. If a child is in immediate danger, call the police first.

What we do
  • We review every report as a priority.
  • We remove content that breaks these standards as soon as we know about it.
  • We close the accounts of people responsible, and stop them from using the service.
  • We report child sexual abuse material and other suspected child exploitation to the Maldives Police Service and any other authority the law requires, and we keep what the law requires us to keep for them.
Laws we follow

We follow the child safety laws that apply to us, and we cooperate with the authorities in any investigation.
