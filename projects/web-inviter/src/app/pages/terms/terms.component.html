Using the service

invites.blog lets you create, personalize, and deliver digital invitations. You’re responsible for the content you create and for having permission to contact the guests on your list.

Acceptable use

Don’t use invites.blog to send spam, unlawful, hateful, or misleading content, or to message people who have not agreed to hear from you. We may suspend campaigns that violate these terms.

Payment

Making invitations, sharing your own links and collecting replies is free. A Party or Wedding pass is paid once, for one event, and covers it for a year. "Keep your photos" is paid a year at a time. Studio is a subscription billed monthly or yearly, and Venue one billed monthly, until you cancel them. Invitations invites.blog emails for you beyond what a pass includes are charged per block of guests. Prices are in Maldivian rufiyaa (MVR); dollar amounts are approximate. The total is shown before you pay.

Photos and storage

Every event can collect photos and videos from its guests. On Free an event holds 1 GB in one album, guests can add from the day before the event to the day after, and the photos are kept for 90 days after the event. A Party or Wedding pass gives the event more space, more albums and more days, and keeps its photos for a year. "Keep your photos" keeps them online for a year more. Free events show a small "Made with invites.blog" on the invitation and its albums.

When an event's cover ends, guests can no longer add anything but can still look for 30 days; then only you can, for another 60 days; after that the photos and videos are removed. We email you before each step. Your invitation, guest list and replies are not removed with them.

Delivery

We work to deliver every invitation reliably, but delivery depends on third-party channels and the accuracy of the contact details you provide. Delivery and RSVP figures are reported on your dashboard on a best-effort basis.

Refunds

A pass or “Keep your photos” can be refunded until guests start adding photos to the event or invitations are sent under it; after that it is not refundable. Invitations we have already sent are not refundable. Studio and Venue can be cancelled at any time and run to the end of the period already paid, which is not refunded. Contact us if something went wrong and we’ll make it right where we can.
