Photos and storage

Every event can collect photos and videos from its guests. On Free an event holds 300 MB in one album, guests can add from the day before the event to the day after, and the photos are kept for good. A Gold or Platinum pass gives the event more space, more albums and more days, and keeps everything for a year. "Keep your photos" keeps everything for a year more. Free events show a small "Made with invites.blog" on the invitation and its albums.

Anyone who can see an album can download or share up to 10 photos and videos at a time. Downloading or sharing more than 10 at once, and "Download all" (the whole album as one zip), need Premium, Photographer or Studio on your own account, or a Gold or Platinum pass on the event.

When a pass (or Premium) ends, the event goes back to Free: guests can no longer add anything, everyone can still look, and up to 300 MB is kept for good. If the event holds more than that, we email you and send a notification at least 30 days before, then 7 days and 1 day before, and only what is above 300 MB is then removed (the cover photos and the earliest uploads are kept). Your invitation, guest list and replies are never removed with them.
