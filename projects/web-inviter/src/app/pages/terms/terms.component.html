Using the service

invites.blog lets you create, personalize, and deliver digital invitations. You’re responsible for the content you create and for having permission to contact the guests on your list.

Acceptable use

Don’t use invites.blog to send spam, unlawful, hateful, or misleading content, or to message people who have not agreed to hear from you. We may suspend campaigns that violate these terms.

Payment

Making invitations, sharing your own links and collecting replies is free. A Party or Wedding pass is paid once, for one event, and covers it for a year. "Keep your photos" is paid a year at a time. Studio and Venue are subscriptions, billed monthly or yearly until you cancel them. Invitations invites.blog emails for you beyond what a pass includes are charged per block of guests. Prices are in Maldivian rufiyaa (MVR); dollar amounts are approximate. The total is shown before you pay.

Delivery

We work to deliver every invitation reliably, but delivery depends on third-party channels and the accuracy of the contact details you provide. Delivery and RSVP figures are reported on your dashboard on a best-effort basis.

Refunds

Because invitations are sent immediately after payment, campaigns that have begun sending are generally non-refundable. Contact us if something went wrong and we’ll make it right where we can.
