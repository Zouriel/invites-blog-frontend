Legal Privacy Policy How invites.blog handles the information you and your guests share with us.
What we collect

To send invitations, we process the guest details you upload (names, and email addresses and phone numbers where you give them), along with the content of your invitation and your host contact details. When guests add photos and videos to an event, we store them with the name the guest gave, and any comments and likes on the event’s page. We also keep the details of your account.

How we use it

Guest information is used solely to deliver your invitations, personalize each one, and report delivery and RSVP status back to you. We do not sell your data or your guests’ data.

Retention

An event’s photos and videos are kept for 90 days after a free event, or a year with a Party or Wedding pass, and a year more each time “Keep your photos” is added. After that they are removed, and we email the host before it happens. The invitation, guest list and replies stay with the event until you delete it. You can request deletion of an event and its guest list at any time.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to deliver invitations (for example, email and payment processors), and only to the extent required to provide the service.

Your choices

Guests can opt out of future messages for a campaign. Hosts can remove guests or cancel a campaign from the dashboard.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at privacy@invites.blog.
