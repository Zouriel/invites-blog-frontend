Legal Privacy Policy How invites.blog handles the information you and your guests share with us.
What we collect

To send invitations, we process the guest details you upload (names, and email addresses and phone numbers where you give them), along with the content of your invitation and your host contact details. When guests add photos and videos to an event, we store them with the name the guest gave, and any comments and likes on the event’s page. We also keep the details of your account (your name, email or phone, and a one-way hash of your password if you set one, never the password itself). Face templates are kept only in albums whose host turned on face grouping (see below).

When you pay, we record what the payment was for, its amount in MVR, its date, its status and the bank's reference for it. We never receive your card number, expiry date or security code: those are entered on Bank of Maldives' own payment page. When you buy the Premium pass or Venue, the bank keeps your card on file for the renewals, and we store only the bank's reference to your customer record there.

When you buy in the Android or iPhone app, Google Play or the App Store takes the payment. We receive a record of what was bought, when, and the store's reference for it, never your card or payment details.

How we use it

Guest information is used solely to deliver your invitations, personalize each one, and report delivery and RSVP status back to you. Your account details are used to sign you in, to email you about your events and payments, and to apply what you have paid for. Payment records are used to deliver what you bought, to show you your billing history, to handle refunds and disputes, and to keep the accounts the law requires. We may also email or message you, as an account holder, about invites.blog news and offers; every such message has a way to opt out. We never send marketing to your guests. We do not sell your data or your guests’ data, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Retention

A free event’s photos and videos are kept for as long as the event exists. With a Gold or Platinum pass everything is kept for a year, and a year more each time “Keep your photos” is added; after that the event goes back to Free and up to 300 MB is kept, while anything above it is removed after we have warned the host at least 30 days ahead. The invitation, guest list and replies stay with the event until you delete it. You can delete an event and its guest list at any time. Payment records are kept for as long as the law requires us to keep business accounts, even if the account is deleted, with no link to you.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to deliver invitations (for example, email and payment processors), and only to the extent required to provide the service. Card payments are made on Bank of Maldives' payment page: we receive whether a payment succeeded and its amount, never your card number.

When a venue runs your event, the venue and its staff can see and manage it like its host: the guest list, replies and the photos in its albums.

Grouping photos by face (biometric data)

This is off unless the host of an album turns it on. When it is on, we find the faces in the album’s photos and turn each face into a string of numbers, a face template. Templates are used only to put photos of the same person together inside that album, so the host and guests can filter by person; the host can give a group a name. We don’t use templates to work out who anyone is outside the album.

Where. It runs on our own server. Faces and templates are never sent to another company or to a cloud AI service.

What we don’t do. We never sell, rent, trade or share templates. We never compare them between albums or accounts. We never use them for advertising or to train models, and nobody can export them.

Your choice. Anyone who can see the album, with or without an account, can tap “That’s me” to opt out. We then delete your face data in that album and keep you out of grouping there from then on. To do that we keep one averaged template of your group, only to leave you out of later photos; it is deleted with the album. You can also email us the album link and which photo is you, and we will do it within 7 days. A parent or guardian can ask the same for a child.

Retention and destruction. Templates are deleted when the first of these happens: the photo is deleted; the album or the account is deleted; the host switches grouping off; the person opts out (only the averaged opt-out template stays, until the album is deleted); or the album has had no new photos or changes for 24 months. They are never kept longer than 3 years after the album’s last activity. They are deleted straight away from our live systems and from backups within 14 days.

Who is responsible. The host decides to switch it on and must tell their guests. We run it for the host, on the host’s instruction.

Face grouping is not offered for events in Illinois, Texas or Washington (USA). To use your choice by email, write to {{ company.email }}.

How we protect it

Everything sent between your browser and invites.blog travels over HTTPS (TLS encryption). Our database is not reachable from the internet, and only our team can reach the systems that hold your data. Only you, and the people you give access to, can see your events, guest lists and photos. Card details never reach our systems at all; the bank's page handles them. More on the Payment security page.

Stored in your browser

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps your sign-in and a few preferences (such as light or dark mode) in its local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes your sign-in.

Your choices

Guests can opt out of future messages for a campaign. Hosts can remove guests or cancel a campaign from the dashboard. To stop news and offers from us, use the opt-out in any such message or ask us at the contact details below; it doesn't affect messages about your events and payments.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from Sign-in & security. It happens straight away and can't be undone. Your account, profile picture, comments and likes are deleted, and so are events and albums with nobody else on them, with their guest lists, replies, photos and videos. An event that's also for other people carries on with everything in it and passes to one of them. Photos and videos you added to other people's events stay there, with the name shown on them, and so does your row on their guest lists. Automatic renewal stops, and payment records stay with no link to you. Full details are on Delete your account.
