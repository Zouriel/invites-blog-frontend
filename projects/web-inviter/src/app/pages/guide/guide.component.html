Save it as an .xlsx file. CSV and old .xls files can’t be read, and only the first sheet is used. The columns

Put these headers in the first row. The order doesn’t matter, and capital letters don’t either, but the spelling does: E-Mail won’t be recognised.

Any other column is kept with the guest as an extra detail. It doesn’t appear on the invitation.

Every guest needs an email or a phone number. A row with neither can’t be sent, so it’s left out. Roles

Roles are the groups you set up on the Roles step, such as Bridesmaids or Groom family men. Each role decides which sections of the invitation a guest sees and which dress colours they’re asked to wear.

If your invitation has roles, every guest needs at least one. You can’t continue until each row names a role you created.
  • Spell each role the way you named it on the Roles step. Capital letters don’t matter, but a role you didn’t create stops that row.
  • A guest can have several roles. Put them all in the one role cell, separated by any of these: @for (s of separators; track s) { {{ s }} } For example Groom family men; Groom family women.
  • A household invited as two roles gets both. They see every section either role sees, and both sets of dress colours.
  • Put the main role first. The first role sets the invitation’s colours when roles are themed differently.
  • Dress colours aren’t a column. Set a palette for each role on the Roles step, and every guest with that role gets it.
  • An uploaded design has no roles. If you uploaded your own picture as the invitation, leave the role column empty or remove it.
A good example Common mistakes
  • Saving as CSV: save as .xlsx instead.
  • Merged cells: the whole file is turned away. Unmerge them and keep one guest per row.
  • Header spelled differently: use email, phone, name, role and gender exactly.
  • No email and no phone: the row is left out. A completely empty row is simply skipped.
  • A mistyped email: it’s ignored. If the row has no phone either, the row is left out.
  • Phone numbers without a country code: pick a default country on the upload page, or start the number with +.
  • The same guest twice: a repeated email or phone number is dropped, and the first row is kept.
  • A missing role: when your invitation has roles, a row without one can’t be saved.
  • A role that doesn’t exist: check the spelling against the Roles step, or add the role there first.
  • A comma or semicolon inside a role name: it splits the role in two. Rename the role on the Roles step, e.g. Groom family (men).
  • Values in the wrong column: keep names, emails and numbers in their own columns.
