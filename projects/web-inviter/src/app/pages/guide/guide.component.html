The one rule: every row must have at least one of email or phone. A good example Common mistakes
  • Empty rows: a row with no email and no phone is skipped.
  • Merged cells: unmerge them and keep one guest per row.
  • Column names: keep them lowercase, like email, not E-Mail.
  • Phone numbers without a country code: pick a default country on the upload page, or start the number with +.
  • Duplicates: the same email or phone number twice is flagged.
  • Values in the wrong column: keep names, emails and numbers in their own columns.
  • Roles: every guest needs at least one role, spelled the way you named it on the Roles step. For several, put them in one cell with a semicolon between them, like Groom family male; Groom family female.
